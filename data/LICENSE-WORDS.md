# Word data license

Spanish word list (`data/words-open/`): created and curated by the repository owner, Fekete Kálmán, with
AI assistance, all rights reserved (see the LICENSE file in the repository root). No third-party word
list. The photo metadata in `data/words-open/images.json` describes third-party images from Wikimedia
Commons, which keep their own licences; see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md.

English word list (`data/words/en/`): AI-generated cards. The B1 and B2 lists follow The CEFR-J Wordlist
Version 1.5, compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies (cefr-j.org), used under its
terms for research and commercial use with attribution (github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj).
The B2 file (`b2.json`) also holds the CEFR-J A1-B1 words that the lower files do not teach.
The C1 list (`data/words/en/c1.json`) is not from a third-party list: it is made of the English headwords
of the repository owner's own Spanish word list (`data/words-open/` B2 and C1, all rights reserved) that
the CEFR-J A1-B2 lists and the lower English levels do not already cover, with the example sentences
and Hungarian glosses of that list as seeds. No other third-party word list is used for C1.
